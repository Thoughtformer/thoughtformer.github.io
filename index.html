About

Experience across systems, media, and people.

My professional background includes leading IT operations, systems administration, enterprise technology, and collaborating with vendors and service providers. I've also worked hands-on in electronics repair and refurbishment, including guiding technicians through practical, often complicated work.

Creative work has long been part of my life, too. I have training in audio engineering and experience in graphic design, photography, videography, and editing. I'm interested in the ways sound, images, design, and technology shape what people notice, understand, and feel.

I enjoy making sense of complex problems, asking questions, and finding ways for people to work and create with fewer barriers. That curiosity extends to data, AI-assisted tools, community-oriented ideas, and the relationship between technological systems and everyday human experience.

I use AI tools in personal projects and describe their contribution openly. I set goals and constraints, test workflows, review results, and guide revisions while continuing to develop my programming and research skills. I try to distinguish clearly between what a project demonstrates and what remains to be learned.
